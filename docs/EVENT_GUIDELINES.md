# Event Overview V2

**EVENT OVERVIEW**

**EVENT TITLE:** *AWS Student Community Day: South Summit 2026 (AWS SCD: SS 2026\)*   
**THEME:** *Cloud x AI: BUILD. POWER. LEAD.*

**I. OVERVIEW**

AWS Student Community Day: South Summit 2026 is a student-centered technology and leadership conference designed to empower the next generation of innovators, builders, and future tech leaders across South Luzon. Organized by the AWS Student Builder Group, the summit aims to create an engaging and inclusive environment where students can explore the evolving landscape of Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, leadership, and career development.trff

With the theme **“Cloud x AI: BUILD. POWER. LEAD.”**, the event will gather approximately 200 students, student leaders, and technology advocates for a full-day immersive experience focused on inspiration, accessibility, innovation, and community.

Unlike traditional highly technical conferences, AWS SCD: South Summit 2026 is designed to balance technical exposure with human-centered experiences. The summit will feature **inspiring keynote sessions, beginner-friendly cloud and AI discussions, women in tech empowerment talks, career development sessions, community networking activities**, and **interactive sponsor booths** that help students identify their first steps into the tech industry.

The event envisions becoming one of the leading student technology gatherings in South Luzon by fostering an ecosystem where students feel empowered, represented, connected, and motivated to pursue careers in technology regardless of their background or skill level.

# **II. RATIONALE**

As cloud computing and artificial intelligence continue to reshape industries worldwide, there is an increasing need to prepare students not only with technical awareness but also with the confidence, adaptability, and community support necessary to thrive in the modern technology landscape.

Despite the growing demand for cloud and AI professionals, many students across regional universities still face barriers such as limited exposure to industry practices, lack of mentorship opportunities, uncertainty in career pathways, and limited access to inclusive technology communities.

AWS SCD: South Summit 2026 addresses these challenges by creating a student-first environment that bridges the gap between academic learning and real-world industry experiences. Through meaningful discussions, practical learning opportunities, and community-driven engagement, the summit aims to make cloud and AI more approachable, relatable, and inspiring for students from diverse backgrounds.

The summit also recognizes the importance of representation and inclusivity in technology. By intentionally highlighting women in tech, student success stories, and accessible career pathways, the event seeks to encourage a more diverse and empowered generation of future builders and innovators.

Ultimately, AWS SCD: South Summit 2026 is not simply a conference about technology—it is a platform that helps students envision their future in tech and realize that they belong in the industry.

# **III. EVENT OBJECTIVES**

The event aims to:

1. Introduce students to emerging trends and opportunities in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.  
2. Inspire students through stories, experiences, and insights from industry professionals, student leaders, and technology advocates.  
3. Provide beginner-friendly and practical learning opportunities related to cloud, AI, leadership, and career development.  
4. Promote diversity, inclusivity, and representation in technology, especially by empowering women in tech and underrepresented communities.  
5. Strengthen collaboration among universities, student organizations, technology communities, and industry partners across South Luzon.  
6. Create meaningful networking opportunities between students, mentors, sponsors, and professionals.  
7. Encourage students to pursue continuous learning, innovation, and community involvement within the technology ecosystem.

* IT, Computer Science, and Engineering students  
* Student leaders and technology communities  
* Women in tech communities  
* Educators and industry partners

To provide a more organized and engaging event experience, AWS SCD: South Summit 2026  will utilize two primary event spaces within the venue: the **Summit Stage** and the **SS Community Hub**.

The **Summit Stage**, located at the 4th Floor Auditorium, will serve as the main venue for the official program proper and major conference activities. This area will host keynote sessions, technical and career development talks, panel discussions, community showcases, and other main-stage experiences throughout the summit.

Meanwhile, the **SS Community Hub**, located on the 2nd Floor, will function as the summit’s interactive networking and engagement space. The area will feature sponsor booths, partner organizations, technology communities, and a dedicated photobooth area designed to encourage collaboration, networking, and community interaction among participants.

By separating the main conference sessions from the community engagement spaces, the event aims to create a balanced and immersive summit experience that promotes both learning and meaningful connection within the student technology ecosystem.

## **Part 2: Departments & Roles**

### **1\. Executive Department**

The Executive Department is the overarching leadership and administrative engine of the summit. This team does not micromanage the other departments; rather, they remove blockers, manage high-level university and government red tape, enforce the master timeline, and ensure the event's high-tier professional standard is never compromised.

* **Assoc Director:** Orchestrates the master project timeline, resolves inter-departmental friction, and holds department heads accountable to their specific KPIs. Acts as the ultimate decision-maker on the ground during the execution phase.  
* **Executive Secretary(2):** The administrative anchor. Tracks high-level cross-department meeting minutes, manages the master deliverable tracker, and coordinates critical paperwork (e.g., venue permits, faculty memorandums) with university administration.

### **2\. Operations Department**

The Operations Department is the logistical backbone of the event. It handles venue setup, materials, transportation, and technical requirements. This department ensures that everything and everyone needed for a successful event is in the right place at the right time—from chairs and microphones to people and transport routes.

> **Note:** Applicants for this department must reside near Laguna.

* **Logistics Officer:** Coordinates the setup, breakdown, and physical arrangements of event spaces. Manages equipment placement, signage installation, crowd flow between venues, and ensures all materials are properly positioned throughout the conference and workshop areas.  
* **Registration Officer:** Handles attendee check-in processes, manages registration desks, distributes event materials and badges, and maintains accurate attendance records. Assists with workshop sign-ups and manages attendee queries during registration periods.  
* **Usher:** Guides attendees throughout the venue, assists with seating arrangements, manages crowd movement between main conference and workshop sessions, and provides directional support. Helps maintain organized transitions during breakout sessions.  
* **Security and Safety Officer:** Ensures venue security and emergency preparedness protocols are followed. Monitors access points, manages crowd safety during high-traffic periods, responds to incidents, and coordinates with venue security for overall event safety.  
* **Technical Officer:** Provides hands-on technical support for audio-visual equipment, troubleshoots technology issues during presentations, assists speakers with equipment setup, and ensures all technical systems function properly throughout the event.  
* **Workshop Assistant:** Supports AWS workshop instructors with hands-on technical assistance during lab sessions. Helps participants with coding challenges, troubleshoots individual technical issues, and ensures smooth workshop execution in computer lab environments.

### **3\. Relations Department**

The Relations Department manages both internal and external communication and partnerships. It takes care of guest relations, public image, sponsor coordination, and organizational networking. This department plays a key role in expanding the event’s reach, building collaborations, and maintaining a professional and welcoming presence.

* **Public Relations Officer:** Manages the organization’s public image. Drafts press releases, external announcements, and works to uphold a positive perception with stakeholders.  
* **Guest Engagement Officer:** Handles all communication and logistics concerning guest speakers, panelists, and VIPs. Prepares itineraries and ensures their experience is smooth.  
* **Partnership Officer:** Builds and maintains partnerships with other organizations, universities, or companies. Prepares proposals and explores collaboration opportunities.  
* **Sponsorship Officer:** Works in coordination with the Finance team to contact and manage sponsors, ensuring requirements and deliverables are fulfilled.  
* **Communications Officer:** Ensures smooth internal communication across departments. Manages group chats, announcements, minutes, and coordination messages.  
* **Documentation Protocol Officer:** Organizes, files, and archives all official documents (meeting notes, reports, attendance, etc.). Shared responsibility with the Marketing Department.

### **4\. Multimedia Department**

The Multimedia Department is responsible for the event’s branding, content creation, promotions, and audience interaction. From designing visuals to curating content strategies and managing social media, the team ensures the event has a strong and consistent presence that resonates with the community—before, during, and after the event.

* **Creatives Head:** Oversees the club’s visual identity, content creation, and media production.  
  * **Layout Artist:** Designs promotional materials such as posters, tarpaulins, slides, and other visual needs for both online and physical events.  
  * **Photographer / Videographer:** Captures key moments before, during, and after events. Works closely with promotions and documentation teams to ensure proper media coverage.  
  * **Multimedia Editor:** Edits photos and videos for content publishing. Prepares highlight reels, teasers, and post-event coverage suitable for social media and presentations.  
  * **Archives Officer:** Maintains and organizes a digital repository of marketing materials and captured media. Ensures everything is well-labeled and accessible for future use.  
* **Marketing Head:** Directs promotional strategies, content calendar, and audience engagement across all platforms.  
  * **Promotions Officer:** Promotes upcoming events and announcements through posters, campaigns, and collaborations. Coordinates timelines with content strategists.  
  * **Content Strategist:** Plans content calendar and messaging. Coordinates what to post, when to post, and which team executes it, ensuring cohesive branding.  
  * **Social Media Manager:** Manages official social platforms. Schedules posts, engages with followers, replies to queries, and monitors performance metrics.  
  * **Audience Engagement Officer:** Focuses on increasing interaction with the audience. Initiates online activities, responds to comments, and gathers feedback to boost engagement.

### **5\. Finance Department**

The Finance Department is the fiscal backbone of the organization and its events. It handles budgeting, fund sourcing, material procurement, and transparency reports. This department ensures that resources are allocated efficiently, expenditures stay within constraints, and every expense is fully accounted for—from structuring the financial side of sponsorship packages to securing merchandise, supplies, and materials, whether purchased from retail stores or external vendors.

* **Budget Officer:** Manages the overall financial planning and allocation for organization initiatives and events. Responsible for creating comprehensive budget proposals, tracking real-time expenses against projected costs, and advising the team on cost-saving measures to prevent overspending.  
* **Sponsorship Officer:** Spearheads fund-sourcing strategies and designs the financial tiers and packages offered to potential partners. Drafts official sponsorship proposals, communicates with corporate or community sponsors, negotiates financial agreements, and ensures that all sponsor deliverables are tracked and fulfilled.  
* **Procurement Officer:** Canvasses, negotiates, and purchases all event merchandise, tokens, and logistical items. Responsible for sourcing reliable production vendors, buying supplies directly from physical or online retail stores, gathering price quotations, and verifying item dimensions or design requirements before finalizing orders. Note: All purchases must be supported by approved purchase requests and remain within the allocated budget approved by the Budget Officer and Finance Director.   
* **Financial Auditor:** Ensures absolute transparency, accuracy, and integrity in all financial records. Reviews receipts, balances the organization's ledgers, cross-checks department expenses against approved budgets, and flags any financial discrepancies or unauthorized spending before reports are finalized.  
* **Liquidation Officer:** Handles the collection, documentation, and official closing of accounts post-event. Gathers all physical store receipts and digital invoices from the team, validates reimbursement requests, formats liquidation reports for organizational compliance, and ensures all cash advances are settled appropriately.  
* **Asset & Inventory Officer:** Maintains and monitors the SCD South 2026’s physical assets, equipment, merchandise, and event materials. Responsible for recording inventory movement, tracking borrowed and returned items, conducting inventory audits before and after the event, and ensuring that assets/resources (SWAGS) are properly stored and maintained.

**6\. Technology Department**

The Technology Department is responsible for the summit's digital infrastructure — primarily the official event website, registration systems, and any technical tools used to support attendee experience before, during, and after the event. This team ensures the digital front-facing side of the summit is functional, secure, and reflects the same professional standard as the physical event.

1. **Web Developer:** Builds and maintains the official event website, including page structure, responsiveness, and integration of registration or ticketing forms. Ensures cross-browser and mobile compatibility.  
2. **UI/UX Designer:** Designs the website's layout and user flow in coordination with the Creatives team's branding guidelines. Focuses on usability, accessibility, and visual consistency across all site pages.  
3. **Systems & Registration Officer:** Manages backend systems tied to attendee registration, data collection, and form submissions. Coordinates with Operations to ensure registration data flows correctly into check-in processes.  
4. **Cybersecurity & Maintenance Officer:** Handles website security, hosting, domain management, and data backups. Monitors for vulnerabilities and ensures uptime throughout the event period.
